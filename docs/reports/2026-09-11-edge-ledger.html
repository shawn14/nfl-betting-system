Moneyline: the win rates are favorites

The system's "moneyline edge" is only how far its own win probability sits from 50%; it never sees the price. So its high-conviction moneyline picks are almost all favorites, and a 70–90% win rate on favorites is what favorites do. Underdog picks tell the real story.

NHL puck line by side

The NHL model takes the +1.5 underdog side in about three of every four games. That side wins about 70% of the time at a typical price near −230, which is roughly break-even, not an edge. The −1.5 favorite side wins about 41% and usually pays +150 to +200, which could be profitable, but no prices are stored, so neither can be judged.

Closing line value

Did the market move toward our pick between the opening line and the locked line about an hour before kickoff? Beating the close is the most reliable early sign of real edge. Negative numbers mean the market moved against us.
